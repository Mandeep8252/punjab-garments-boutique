# Punjab Garments Premier

Build a modern, classy, single-page advertisement/landing website for my clothing shop, "Punjab Garments." The site will be linked from our Google Maps listing, so it should load fast and feel premium on mobile.

BRAND & VIBE:

- Style: modern, classy, elegant boutique feel — think soft neutrals (cream, blush, deep maroon or gold accents) with elegant serif or modern sans-serif typography.

- Smooth, "sexy" animations throughout: scroll-triggered fade/slide-ins, a subtle parallax or animated gradient/image in the hero, smooth hover effects on product cards (slight zoom/lift), and smooth page-load transitions. Keep animations tasteful and fast, not gimmicky.

SECTIONS (in order):

1. HERO BANNER

- Full-width hero with an elegant animated entrance (text fades/slides in, background image with subtle zoom or parallax motion).

- Headline: "Punjab Garments" with a tagline like "Elegant Women's Fashion, Made for You"

- A call-to-action button: "Visit Us" that scrolls down to the address/hours section.

2. OUR COLLECTION / PRODUCTS

- A section titled "What We Offer" showcasing our product categories with images in an elegant grid or carousel with hover zoom animations:

  - Kurti Sets

  - Nayra Cuts

  - Gowns

  - Suits

  - Crop Tops

  - Jeans Tops

- Use placeholder fashion images for now (I'll replace with real photos later); each card should have the category name and a short elegant one-line description.

- Cards should animate into view on scroll (staggered fade-up).

3. ABOUT US

- Section with owner details: "Meet the Owner"

- Owner name: Amandeep Singh

- A short warm paragraph about the shop's passion for quality women's fashion and personal service (write elegant placeholder copy I can edit).

4. VISIT US (Address & Hours)

- Address: Kapra Patti Bhunja Wala Gali, Gudari Bazar, Takkad Morde, Chapra, Bihar 841301, India

- Opening Hours: Open all week, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM

- Include an embedded Google Map showing this location.

- Include a "Get Directions" button linking to Google Maps.

5. CONTACT / FOOTER

- Phone number: 8252439516 with a clickable "Call Now" button and WhatsApp click-to-chat button.

- Simple footer with shop name and a closing line like "Thank you for choosing Punjab Garments."

TECHNICAL:

- Fully responsive, mobile-first (most visitors will come from a phone via Google Maps).

- Fast-loading, clean code, smooth scrolling enabled site-wide.

- Sticky/floating "Call Now" button on mobile for easy access.
